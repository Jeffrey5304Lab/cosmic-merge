Privacy Policy — Cosmic Merge

Last updated: 9 August 2026

Cosmic Merge ("the game", "we") is a casual planet-merging game. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the game and an optional global leaderboard. This page explains what is collected, why, and your choices.

1. What stays on your device

The game stores the following in your device's local storage. It never leaves your device, except for the optional leaderboard submission described below:

  • Your best score, lifetime stats, and unlocked achievements
  • Your chosen display name and country (remembered for convenience)
  • Sound on/off preference and in-game item count

You can clear all of it at any time by clearing the app's data / website storage.

2. The optional global leaderboard

When a game ends you may submit your result to a public leaderboard. If you do, the following is sent and stored on our database host:

  • A display name you type (optional; defaults to "Anonymous")
  • A country you select from a list (optional; this is self-selected, not GPS or IP-based location)
  • Your score and highest planet reached

This information is shown publicly on the in-game leaderboard. Please do not enter real names or any personal information you do not wish to be public. We do not collect your email, account, contacts, precise location, photos, or device identifiers.

3. Advertising

The game offers optional rewarded video ads: you may choose to watch a short ad to continue a run ("revive") or to earn a hammer. Ads are never forced, and no part of the game is locked behind watching one. Ads are served by Google AdMob.

To deliver ads, Google may collect and process device and usage information, including advertising identifiers, as described in Google's policies. On iOS 14.5+ the game asks your permission (Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt) before any cross-app tracking; if you decline, ads still work but are non-personalized. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

4. Third-party services

The game relies on the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — hosts the optional public leaderboard entries described above.
  • Google AdMob — serves the optional rewarded ads described above. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

No other analytics or tracking SDKs are used.

5. Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only data you provide directly is a self-chosen display name and country, which you control and can leave blank. Ads (section 3) are served by Google AdMob and may involve advertising identifiers as described in Google's policies.

6. Data retention & deletion

On-device data is removed when you clear the app's storage. To request removal of a leaderboard entry, contact us at the address below with enough detail (name + score) to identify it.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
